工程快测隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

工程快测由中国独立开发者梁柏林运营。本政策适用于 iOS 和 iPadOS 版，包括向新加坡、马来西亚和文莱用户提供的版本。隐私事项请联系 Ldeguo2026@outlook.com。

本机项目和权限

项目名称、备注、地址、现场照片、缺陷标注、空间测量和报告初稿默认保存在设备上。应用在设备上扫描、识别、计算和生成文件，不设开发者账号或云项目库，也不会把项目内容自动上传给开发者。应用不集成广告、行为分析或跟踪 SDK。

相机用于扫描和拍照。使用期间的位置权限为可选，用于地点和方向参考。您可在系统设置撤回权限。拒绝相机会限制扫描和拍照。拒绝位置后仍可手工填写地址。应用使用设备姿态辅助确定方向。

苹果服务与主动分享

购买、续费、恢复和凭证校验由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用不会接收银行卡信息或 Apple 账户密码。应用把已验证的应用交易标识、首次获取日期和校验时间缓存在设备钥匙串，用于体验期限和离线校验。正式商店版提供 14 天全功能体验，727 起的 TestFlight 公测版采用两个自然月的单独体验期。无需订阅即可体验，体验到期不扣费。钥匙串记录可能在卸载后保留。

主动查询地址时，系统地址服务可能向 Apple 发送用于查询的位置。开启帮助或隐私网页时，网站托管方 GitHub 可能处理访问所需的 IP 地址等技术信息。主动导出或分享时，您选择的应用、存储服务或接收方会收到所选文件。Apple、GitHub 和您选择的服务依各自的隐私政策处理信息。

客服邮件、保存和您的选择

联系支持是自愿的。开发者从您发来的邮件接收邮箱地址、正文和您自愿附上的资料，用于答复、排查问题和跟进。建议不要附上不必要的联系人、项目地址或客户资料。邮件由邮箱服务商 Microsoft 处理，开发者在中国查阅，服务商可能在其他司法辖区处理。客服记录仅保留至处理请求、必要跟进及履行适用义务所需的期限。

可在应用内删除本机项目，已分享的副本需在接收位置删除。订阅或体验到期不会删除项目。您可向上述邮箱申请访问、更正或删除开发者持有的客服资料，询问处理情况或提出投诉。开发者可为核实请求者身份要求必要信息，并按适用法律处理请求。

Engineering Quick Measure Privacy Notice

Updated 17 November 2026

Engineering Quick Measure (工程快测) is operated by Bolin Liang, an independent developer based in China. This notice covers the iOS and iPadOS app, including versions offered to users in Singapore, Malaysia and Brunei Darussalam. Contact Ldeguo2026@outlook.com for privacy matters.

On-device projects and permissions

Project names, notes, addresses, site photos, defect annotations, measurements and draft reports are stored on your device by default. Scanning, recognition, calculations and document generation run on the device. The app has no developer-operated accounts or cloud project library and does not automatically upload project contents to the developer. It uses no advertising, behavioural analytics or tracking SDKs.

Camera access supports scanning and photography. Optional location access while using the app supports address and direction references. You can withdraw permissions in system settings. Turning off camera access limits scanning and photography. You can enter addresses manually without location access. Device orientation helps determine direction.

Apple services and sharing

Apple StoreKit handles purchases, renewals, restoration and verification. The app does not receive card details or your Apple Account password. The app caches the verified app transaction identifier, date of first acquisition and verification time in the device Keychain to check access offline. The public App Store version offers 14 days of full access. TestFlight beta builds from 727 use a separate two-calendar-month period. No subscription is required for the evaluation and it does not charge at expiry. Keychain records may survive uninstalling the app.

When you request an address lookup, Apple's system service may receive the location needed for the lookup. GitHub, the website host, may process technical information such as IP addresses when you open help or privacy pages. If you export or share files, the app, storage service or recipient you choose receives the selected files. Apple, GitHub and your chosen services handle information under their own privacy policies.

Support email, retention and your choices

Contacting support is voluntary. We receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, diagnose issues and follow up. Please omit unnecessary personal contacts, site addresses and client records. Microsoft provides the email service. The developer accesses messages from China and the service provider may process them in other jurisdictions. We retain support records as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request and meet applicable obligations.

You can delete local projects in the app and delete exported copies at their destinations. Subscription or evaluation expiry does not delete your projects. Use the contact email to ask about our processing, request access to, correction or deletion of support data we hold, or make a complaint. We may request information needed to verify your identity and handle requests under applicable law.
